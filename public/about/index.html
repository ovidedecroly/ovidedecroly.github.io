Dr. Ovide Decroly Wisnu Ardhi

Dr. Ovide Decroly Wisnu Ardhi is an Applied AI Researcher with academic interests in artificial intelligence, computer vision, deep learning, object detection, academic writing, and digital education.

This website, Ovide Academy, is developed as a personal academic platform for sharing research notes, teaching materials, blog articles, books, and applied AI ideas.

Research Interest

Dr. Ovide Decroly Wisnu Ardhi works on applied AI topics, especially those related to visual intelligence and computer vision. His research interests include deep learning, object detection, scientific image analysis, AI-based decision support systems, and the development of educational materials for artificial intelligence and digital technology.
